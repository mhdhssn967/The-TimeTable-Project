# The TimeTabler — Market Potential & Sizing

---

## 1. The Core Market Gap

Most schools in India face two distinct scheduling challenges:
1. **Master Timetabling (1–2× a year):** Usually done manually on whiteboards or Excel (taking 1–3 weeks for 2–3 senior teachers) or with legacy desktop software like **aSc TimeTables** (Windows desktop installation, steep learning curve, localized Indian tiers around ₹10,700–₹50,300+ one-time plus optional annual cloud fees).
2. **Daily 7:30 AM Proxy / Substitution Rush (200+ school days a year):** Even schools with a full School ERP (₹50–₹300+/student/year) or desktop timetable software often fall back to a physical register or manual Excel sheet at 7:30 AM because ERP timetable modules are clunky and desktop tools live on a single office PC.

**The TimeTabler's Wedge:** A cloud-based **daily operational co-pilot** built specifically for Indian school constraints (simultaneous language splits, Plus Two stream electives, lab double periods) without forcing schools to replace their existing systems.

## 3. Kerala Market Potential (Launch Beachhead)

Kerala is an ideal launchpad for **The TimeTabler** due to its high density of multi-division schools, complex second/third language splits (Malayalam, Hindi, Arabic, Sanskrit, French running simultaneously), and high digital literacy among coordinators.

| Segment in Kerala | Count / Estimate | Buyer Profile & Adoption Dynamics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CBSE Schools** | **1,200+** | **Tier-1 Target:** Privately managed, fast decisions, strong pain around language splits and daily substitutions. |
| **ICSE / ISC Schools** | **~150 – 160** | **Tier-1 Target:** Similar to CBSE with high section density and complex electives. |
| **Private Unaided (State Syllabus)** | **~1,000+** | **Tier-1/2 Target:** Independently funded, fast purchasing cycle, handles High School & Plus Two streams. |

## 4. Realistic Milestones (SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market)

In Kerala, many schools operate under **corporate educational managements** (e.g., diocesan school boards, MES, NSS, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Chinmaya Vidyalayas). Signing a single management trust can unlock **10 to 50+ schools at once**.

- **Year 1 (Kerala Beachhead — ~5% of Private CBSE/ICSE/Unaided Schools):**
  - **100 – 150 schools** at ₹12,000 – ₹15,000/year = **₹15 Lakh – ₹22.5 Lakh ARR**
- **Year 2 (Kerala Deepening + Corporate Managements + Early College Pilots):**
  - **400 – 500 schools + ~50 colleges** = **₹75 Lakh – ₹90 Lakh ARR**
- **Year 3 (South India + GCC Indian Schools Multiplier):**
  - *GCC Multiplier:* Indian/CBSE/Kerala-syllabus schools across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, and Kuwait—many operated by Kerala-linked managements—represent a high-ARPU expansion segment (typically paying **3×–5× Indian pricing**, ~₹40,000 – ₹75,000/year).
  - **1,200+ Indian schools + 100 GCC schools + 200 colleges** = **₹3+ Crore ARR**

## 5. Key Strategic Takeaways

1. **Flat Per-School SaaS Pricing Wins Fast Approval:** Charging per student (₹100–₹300/student/yr) forces lengthy trustee/board reviews. A flat **₹999 – ₹1,499/month** (billed annually) keeps the purchase inside the Principal's/Coordinator's discretionary budget.
2. **Lead With the 7:30 AM Proxy Engine for Retention:** Timetable generation alone happens once a year (high churn risk). Daily morning substitution management makes the Vice-Principal open the app 200+ days a year.
3. **FYUGP / NEP 2020 Is a Natural Bridge to Colleges:** Multi-disciplinary major/minor pathways under NEP 2020 and Kerala's Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) create severe manual timetable clashes—positioning Phase 2 college expansion well.
