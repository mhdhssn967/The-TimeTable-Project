# The TimeTabler — Project Overview

## What It Is
A cloud-based **Automated School Timetable & Daily Substitution ("Proxy") Management SaaS**.
- **Starting Focus:** K–12 schools (tailored for Indian & Kerala school workflows).
- **Future Expansion:** Colleges and higher education institutions.
- **Primary Users:** School Academic Coordinators and Vice-Principals.

## The Core Value
Most timetable tools are only used once a year. **The TimeTabler** works as a **daily operational co-pilot**:
1. Turns weeks of manual master timetable scheduling into minutes.
2. Automates the stressful **7:30 AM morning rush** to arrange substitute ("proxy") teachers when staff call in sick.

---

## Core Features

### 1. 1-Click Excel Import & Clash-Free Timetable Generator
- **Easy Upload:** Import the school's existing teacher–subject allocation Excel sheet directly.
- **Zero Conflicts:** Automatically generates a 100% clash-free master timetable in minutes.
- **No Double-Booking:** Ensures no teacher, classroom, or lab is ever double-booked.

### 2. Smart K–12 & Indian/Kerala School Rules
- **Language & Stream Splits:** Handles simultaneous class splits where one division separates into multiple groups at the same time (e.g., Malayalam / Hindi / Arabic second-language splits, or Plus Two Science / Commerce electives).
- **Double Periods:** Locks consecutive double periods for practical labs and Physical Education (PE).
- **Smart Subject Placement:** Avoids putting heavy subjects (like Math and Science) in the tiring post-lunch / last periods.
- **Teacher Burnout Protection:** Caps consecutive back-to-back classes so teachers get breathing room.

### 3. 7:30 AM Daily Proxy / Substitution Engine *(Daily Hook)*
- **Quick Absentee Marking:** When a teacher calls in sick in the morning, the coordinator marks them absent in seconds.
- **Smart Substitute Recommendations:** Automatically suggests and assigns free teachers based on:
  - Subject and class familiarity.
  - Fair daily and weekly workload balance across staff.

### 4. Instant Print-Ready Outputs
- **Print-Ready Exports (PDF & Excel):** Clean layouts ready to print and paste on classroom doors and staffroom noticeboards.
