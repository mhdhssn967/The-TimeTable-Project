## 3. Available Customer Pool (Overall India vs. Kerala)

### A. Overall India Available Customers
- **Private Unaided K–12 Schools (UDISE+ 2024–25):** **3,39,583 schools** total (out of ~14.71 Lakh total schools in India).
  - **Prime National-Board Segment:** **~28,960 – 33,000+ CBSE schools** plus ICSE/ISC schools.
  - **Targetable Mid/Large Private K–12 Pool:** **~85,000 – 1,00,000 schools** with multiple sections per grade.
- **Higher Education (AISHE, Phase 2 Expansion):** **48,246 Colleges** and **1,289 Universities** (with ~15,000 – 20,000 colleges facing acute NEP 2020 scheduling complexity).

## 4. Why Existing Tools Leave an Open Gap

1. **Desktop Tools (`aSc TimeTables`, `FET`)** live on a single coordinator's PC and are hard to use at 7:30 AM when multiple teachers are calling in sick.
2. **Full ERPs (`Entab`, `Fedena`, `SkoolBeep`, `Passdaily`, `Linways`)** cost ₹50,000+/year or per-student fees and force schools to overhaul fees, attendance, and parent apps—yet their timetable generators are often rigid, causing coordinators to revert to manual Excel.
3. **Global Cloud Schedulers (`Prime Timetable`, `TimetableMaster Pro`)** charge USD pricing (~$399+/year) and lack Kerala-specific ergonomics like simultaneous Malayalam/Hindi/Arabic language splits and Plus Two stream pairing out of the box.
