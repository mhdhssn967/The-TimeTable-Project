## 2. How the Founding 100 Schools Offer Works

1. **Zero Risk & High Urgency (Before Year-End):** Schools implementing before the end of this year lock in the **₹8,999** setup & onboarding price.
2. **Double Academic Coverage:** The first 100 schools get **this academic year + the full next academic year** included under the same ₹8,999 payment—making the effective first-year cost negligible while getting them through two timetable cycles and a full year of 7:30 AM daily proxy usage.
3. **Lifetime Founding Perks:** Any future product updates, enhancements, and integrations shipped down the line are added **at zero extra cost** for those first 100 customers.
4. **No-Brainer Renewal (₹2,999/year):** At **~₹250/month** (₹2,999/year), the renewal stays well within a Principal's or Coordinator's discretionary approval limit—making churn minimal once daily proxy workflows become a morning habit.

## 3. Competitive Price Advantage

- **Vs. Legacy Desktop Software (`aSc TimeTables`):** Localized Indian tiers cost ~₹10,700 (Standard) to ₹50,300+ (Pro) upfront without a built-in cloud daily proxy co-pilot. At **₹8,999**, The TimeTabler is cheaper upfront, cloud-based, and includes two academic years for the first 100 schools.
- **Vs. Full School ERPs (`Entab`, `Fedena`, `SkoolBeep`, etc.):** Full ERPs charge ₹50,000+/year or ₹50–₹300+ per student annually and force schools to migrate fees, attendance, and parent apps. The TimeTabler charges a flat, transparent per-school price with zero ERP lock-in.
- **Vs. Global Cloud Schedulers (`TimetableMaster Pro`, `Prime Timetable`):** Foreign USD plans cost ~$399+/year (~₹33,000+/year). The TimeTabler's **₹2,999/year renewal** is less than 1/10th the cost while supporting Indian/Kerala school rules natively.
